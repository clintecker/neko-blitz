## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-19

Initial source snapshot prepared for public release.

- Thirty-One house rules with four starting lives, legal opening knocks, lowest-hand payouts, and configurable low ties.
- Solo bots, friends' rooms, revision-controlled room updates, host termination, and guest replacement by bots.
- Strategy coach, public-card inference, defensive discards, and reproducible strategy studies.
- Illustrated neon lobby with interactive crew, animated card fans, marquee, and responsive controls.
- Phone and tablet layouts, visible card transfer animations, a first-run practice hand, and reduced-motion support.
- Procedural synth music with varied harmonies, drones, glass effects, and persistent mixer settings.
- Project documentation, asset attribution, licensing, contribution templates, and CI configuration.

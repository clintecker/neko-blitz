# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss ideas and code constructively, welcome different experience levels, and give people room to learn.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related interactions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or block accounts when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate to the conduct and protect the people affected.

Report a concern privately to the maintainer using a contact method on the [clintecker profile](https://github.com/clintecker). Include links and context, and avoid posting private details publicly. Reports will be handled with care. Anyone involved in a report should avoid retaliation.
